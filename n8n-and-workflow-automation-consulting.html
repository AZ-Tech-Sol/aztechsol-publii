n8n (pronounced “n-eight-n”) is an open-source workflow automation platform — think Zapier, but more powerful and with flexible pricing. You can self-host it (free) or use their cloud service. It connects to 400+ apps and lets you build complex automations visually or with code.

[/fusion_toggle][fusion_toggle title="Why n8n instead of Zapier or Make?" open="no"]

Zapier and Make charge per task/operation, which gets expensive fast. Self-hosted n8n has no per-task fees — you pay only for hosting. It’s also more powerful: custom code, AI nodes, complex branching, and your data on your own servers. For most businesses doing serious automation, n8n saves 40–70% over Zapier.

[/fusion_toggle][fusion_toggle title="Do I need my own server?" open="no"]

For self-hosted n8n, yes — but a basic cloud server ($10–20/month from DigitalOcean or Hetzner) is plenty for most small businesses, and we handle the entire setup. Alternatively, n8n offers a cloud plan starting at $24/month.

[/fusion_toggle][fusion_toggle title="Can you work with our existing Zapier workflows?" open="no"]

Absolutely. We’ll audit your current Zapier setup, identify which workflows to migrate, and handle the transition. Most clients see no disruption during the switch.
