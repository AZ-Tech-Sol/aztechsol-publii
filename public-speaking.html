Why me

I am Angel Menendez, founder of AZ Technology Solutions and former Staff Developer Advocate at n8n. I have spent 20+ years at the intersection of cybersecurity, automation, and AI: SOAR playbooks at Palo Alto Networks, AI-agent infrastructure at n8n, and production automation for organizations that cannot afford “AI that demos.”
