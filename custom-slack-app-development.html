[fusion_title size="2" content_align="center" text_color="#ffffff"]See it in action: Pokébus[/fusion_title][fusion_text]

Pokébus is a co-op Pokémon-style adventure living inside Slack (the whole workspace boards one bus and rides together) — wild spawns in your channels, one-tap catching with an animated reveal, gym battles, trading, co-op raids, and a Game Boy-style menu opened from a global shortcut. It’s a working, playable demo of what a custom Slack app can really do. (There’s a second game in the Slack too — Idle Realms, a co-op D&D-style channel crawler, but that one is still in the works! Think of it as a live beta.)
