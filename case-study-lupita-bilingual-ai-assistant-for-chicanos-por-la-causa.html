The challenge: a chatbot nobody used

Chicanos Por La Causa is one of the largest Hispanic-serving nonprofits in the country — 2,000+ employees across shelters, charter schools, housing, and workforce programs throughout Arizona. That scale means a constant flood of the same HR and IT questions: How much PTO do I have? How do I enroll in benefits? How do I get a loaner laptop?

CPLC had already tried a chatbot. It was buried on a SharePoint page few people found, gave vague answers, pointed to broken links, and mostly told employees to go find it themselves. Adoption was low and the HR team’s workload never changed.
