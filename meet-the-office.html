AZ Tech runs on a small team of AI “staff” — each one a specialist with a single job it does well, that hands off anything outside its lane. They’re named after characters from The Office (a show we love). Some are already on the clock; others are being hired in over the coming weeks. Think of this as the org chart for the company I’m building.
