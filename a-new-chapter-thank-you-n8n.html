My n8n journey began the way the best stories do — as a curious user. Back in 2022, I discovered n8n and immediately fell in love with the platform. The open-source philosophy, the power, the flexibility — it was everything I'd been looking for in an automation tool.

I was so passionate about it that I started an unofficial n8n podcast, covering new releases, use cases, and the growing community. You can check out the early episodes right here on this blog:

What started as a passion project turned into something bigger. The n8n team noticed. We started talking. And eventually, they offered me a role that combined everything I love — automation, community, developer education, and being at the forefront of AI-powered workflows.

I couldn't say no.

With this new full-time role, I've made the difficult decision to pause active operations at AZ Technology Solutions. This wasn't easy. This company has been my baby — built from the ground up, serving amazing clients across Tucson, Sahuarita, and Green Valley.

But this opportunity at n8n is once-in-a-career. Automation and AI are reshaping every industry, and this role puts me right at the cutting edge of that transformation. I owe it to myself — and ultimately to you, my clients — to grow and learn at this level.
