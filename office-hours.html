A free, open door, no pitch, no catch. Office Hours is a place, not just a calendar: a Slack channel in my private Slack workspace where I build in the open, answer questions, and help you work through whatever you’re stuck on. Join the room, hang out, and book live 1:1 time whenever you want it, the booking link is pinned right inside the Slack channel to make access to me frictionless. If you're already using slack at work, add my workspace and channel to join the community.
