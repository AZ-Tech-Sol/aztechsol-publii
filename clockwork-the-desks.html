Clockwork

A set of automated desks that run your back office.

Every business has the same handful of desks. Someone answers first, someone bills, someone makes sure nothing gets dropped. Clockwork is those desks, running as machines — each one narrow, each one reliable, each one watched.

These desks are automated. They are not people, and they do not pretend to be. Each one has a defined job, and anything outside that job comes to Angel.
